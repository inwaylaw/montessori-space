# Research

This folder collects public, synthetic-only research artifacts for Montessori Space.

- `model-selection/`: general Chinese multimodal evaluation set, OpenRouter budget/redline runner, and model-selection notes.
- `montessori-frame-eval/`: Montessori-specific synthetic frame tasks and early evaluation findings.

OpenRouter results are useful for comparing model capability, but they are cloud-assisted research evidence. They do not mean Montessori Space has completed local-only deployment.
